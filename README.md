## About the Series

**Squid Game** is a South Korean survival thriller series. It is about people with heavy debts who join a secret contest of children's games. The winner gets a huge cash prize, but the losers pay with their lives.

## Main Characters

- Seong Gi-hun (Player 456)
- Cho Sang-woo (Player 218)
- Kang Sae-byeok (Player 067)
- Oh Il-nam (Player 001)
- The Front Man

## Why I Like It

1. The story is tense and full of surprises.
2. The games look simple but become very dangerous.
3. It shows how money problems can push people to make hard choices.

> A story about survival, greed, and what people will do to change their lives.
